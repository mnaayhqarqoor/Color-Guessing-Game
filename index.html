My name is Ahmed. I am eleven years old. I am in grade six. I go to Al-Noor Primary School. My school is big and beautiful. It has a large playground, a library, and a science lab.

Passage 2 A Trip to the Zoo

اقرأ النص التالي ثم أجب عن الأسئلة

A Trip to the Zoo

Last Friday, my class went on a trip to the city zoo. We went by bus early in the morning. The weather was sunny and warm. All the students were very excited.

First, we saw the lions. They were big and strong. The lions were sleeping under a tree. Then we visited the monkey house. The monkeys were funny! They were jumping from tree to tree and playing with each other.

After that, we saw the elephants. They were eating leaves and drinking water. One baby elephant was playing with its mother. It was very cute!

At lunchtime, we sat under a big tree and ate our sandwiches. Our teacher, Mr. Ali, told us interesting facts about animals. We learned that elephants are the largest land animals and that monkeys are very smart.

We went home at 3:00 PM. It was the best day ever!

Passage 3 The Importance of Water

اقرأ النص التالي ثم أجب عن الأسئلة

The Importance of Water

Water is one of the most important things in our lives. We need water to drink, to cook, and to clean. Without water, we cannot live.

There are many sources of water. Rivers, lakes, and seas are natural sources of water. We also get water from rain. Some countries do not have enough water, so they must save it carefully.

We should not waste water. When we brush our teeth, we should turn off the tap. We should take short showers instead of long baths. We should also fix any leaks in our houses.

Plants and animals need water too. Trees need water to grow. Fish live in water. If we pollute the water, many animals will die. That is why we must keep our water clean.

Remember: every drop of water is precious. Let's save water for our future!

Part 1 Present Simple Tense

المضارع البسيط - يُستخدم للعادات والحقائق

📌 القاعدة / Rule:

نستخدم المضارع البسيط للتحدث عن العادات والحقائق اليومية. مع الضمائر (He, She, It) نضيف s/es للفعل.
I go to school every day.
She goes to school every day.
They play football.
He plays football.

Part 2 Past Simple Tense

الماضي البسيط - يُستخدم للأحداث التي حدثت وانتهت في الماضي

📌 القاعدة / Rule:

نستخدم الماضي البسيط للتحدث عن أحداث حدثت في الماضي. الأفعال المنتظمة نضيف ed، والأفعال الشاذة لها تصريف خاص.
I played football yesterday. (منتظم)
She went to school yesterday. (شاذ - go → went)
They ate lunch. (شاذ - eat → ate)
He was happy. (شاذ - is/am → was)

Part 2 Prepositions of Place & Time

📌 القاعدة / Rule:

حروف الجر تستخدم لبيان المكان والزمان
in - inside something: in the box, in the room
on - on a surface: on the table, on the wall
at - a specific point: at school, at home
under - below something: under the chair
between - in the middle: between two things

Part 2 Question Words

📌 القاعدة / Rule:

كلمات الاستفهام تستخدم للسؤال عن معلومات محددة
What - للسؤال عن الأشياء: What is your name?
Where - للسؤال عن المكان: Where do you live?
When - للسؤال عن الوقت: When is your birthday?
Who - للسؤال عن الأشخاص: Who is your teacher?
How - للسؤال عن الطريقة: How do you go to school?
Why - للسؤال عن السبب: Why are you sad?
